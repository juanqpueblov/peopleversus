# Welcome to *The People Versus*

**The People Versus** is about people power — the kind that grows when communities organize and act together for the common good.

Power itself isn’t the problem. It’s a necessary force — what makes things move.  
The problem is when power is concentrated, when it’s used to protect privilege or profit instead of being shared to serve the public.  

That’s where we come in.

We dig into public records, court filings, and policy decisions not just to reveal facts, but to help people see the patterns — how decisions are shaped, whose voices are included or ignored, and what stories are being told to justify the status quo.

Our goal isn’t only to expose “bad actors,” but to strengthen the understanding that **power can and should belong to everyone** — in workplaces, schools, and communities.  
By questioning dominant narratives and following the paper trail, we aim to make organizing and collective action better informed, more strategic, and more connected.  We aim to bring to light that which is normally hidden.

Here you’ll find:
- **Investigations** into how public institutions and corporations wield influence.  
- **Analysis** of the narratives that shape public opinion and policy.  
- **Stories of people organizing for change** and expanding democratic participation.  
- **Resources** for transparency and collective action.

We represent people who believe that power grows when it’s shared, and that truth should serve our collective action.

Because in the end, the goal is about learning to build power and use it — together, for the common good.
